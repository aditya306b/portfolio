## Context

The finance tracker stores a few hundred small records, offline, on one device.

## Options

1. **AsyncStorage**: trivial API, zero setup.
2. **SQLite**: queries and migrations, more code.

## Decision

AsyncStorage. The data is small and I can swap it later behind one storage module.

## Consequences

- Fast to ship.
- Reporting over large histories will be slower; revisit when a backend arrives.
